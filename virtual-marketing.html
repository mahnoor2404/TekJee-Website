Digital Marketing is online marketing delivered remotely by an outside team. TEKJEE plans, runs and reports on social media, email, content, SEO and paid ad campaigns without you hiring in-house marketers.

Which channels does TEKJEE manage?

TEKJEE manages social media, email marketing, content marketing, SEO and paid advertising, and reports on all of them together.

Yes. TEKJEE offers SEO, Local SEO and Technical SEO alongside digital marketing, so nearby customers can find your business in search — and a clear, answer-first content structure that also helps you get cited by AI assistants (GEO, Generative Engine Optimization).

Can I start with just one channel?

Yes. Many clients start with social media or email and add paid ads, SEO and content later. TEKJEE scopes the work to what you choose.
