AI that takes on a role

Tell it who to be. Give it your files. It does the job.

TEKJEE AI adapts to the character you need. Ask it to act as a lawyer, upload your contracts and case files, and it reads them, extracts the key information and answers in that role. The same AI can work as your accountant, recruiter or support agent.

Acts as a lawyer

Reads contracts, agreements and case files, pulls out parties, dates, obligations and risky clauses, and answers your legal questions about them.

Acts as an accountant

Goes through invoices, receipts and statements, extracts amounts, vendors and due dates, and summarises spending.

Acts as a recruiter

Screens CVs against your job description, lists each candidate’s skills and experience, and shortlists the best matches.

Acts as your support agent

Learns your product guides and policies, then answers customers in your tone and hands complex cases to your team.
