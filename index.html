FAQ

Frequently asked questions about TEKJEE

What is TEKJEE?

TEKJEE is a digital solutions company that provides digital marketing, AI automation, web development, back office support, CRM automation, SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), and camera monitoring through a single team.

What services does TEKJEE offer?

TEKJEE offers seven services: digital marketing, AI automation, web development, back office support, CRM automation, SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), and camera monitoring.

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) helps a website rank in search engines such as Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) helps a business get named and cited in answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. TEKJEE offers both.

Who is TEKJEE for?

TEKJEE is for businesses that want digital marketing, websites, AI automation, CRM automation, back office support and camera monitoring handled by one team instead of several separate vendors.

How does a TEKJEE project work?

Every TEKJEE project follows four steps: assess your needs, design and build the solution, deploy it into your tools or premises, then monitor and improve it after launch.

What is Virtual Camera Monitoring, and which businesses is it for?

Virtual Camera Monitoring is live, remote video surveillance of a business's premises, with 24/7 camera monitoring, AI video monitoring and motion alerts. TEKJEE sets this up for gas stations, retail stores, warehouses, restaurants, hotels and motels, with secure cloud or NVR storage.

What is AI Automation, and what does it include?

AI Automation is agentic AI, AI bots and a Virtual Manager that TEKJEE builds and connects to a business, plus AI workflow automation for marketing, sales and operations. It removes repetitive work without replacing the team doing it.
