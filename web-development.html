FAQ

Web development questions, answered

What is an SEO and GEO ready website?

It is a website built so search engines can rank it (SEO) and AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity can understand and cite it (GEO). TEKJEE adds both while the site is being built, not as an afterthought.

What is GEO, and why does my website need it?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) structures your content and data so AI tools recommend your business in their answers. More customers now ask AI for suggestions instead of searching, so a GEO-ready site reaches them too.

How long does it take to build a website?

A business website usually takes 3–6 weeks and a landing page about 1–2 weeks. E-commerce stores and web apps are scoped individually, with weekly progress check-ins.

Can TEKJEE redesign my site without losing Google rankings?

Yes. TEKJEE maps every old URL, sets up redirects and keeps your existing SEO signals, then improves speed, structure and schema on the new site.

Which platforms does TEKJEE build on?

TEKJEE builds custom-coded sites and works with WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce and headless setups, choosing whatever fits your goals, budget and team.

Do you offer hosting and maintenance after launch?

Yes. TEKJEE offers care plans covering hosting, security, backups, updates, speed tuning and monthly SEO and GEO checks.
